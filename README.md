### Cows are **NOT** infinite milk machines!
In Minecraft, Cows can give **endless** amounts of Milk, <br>
and drinking it does **nothing** for your hunger. That, frankly, makes **no** sense! <br>
#### *_Cows are hungry and milk fills you up!_*
